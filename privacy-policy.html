Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

Chromium Games ("we", "us", "the Site") is a free browser games portal operated by Ajdin. For any privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact us at: ajdin.hasic56@gmail.com.

2. Data We Store (Local Storage Only)

Chromium Games does not require an account and does not store personal data on our servers. All of your data is stored locally in your own browser using localStorage, and it never leaves your device through us. This includes:

  • Favorites — which games you starred (favorite_games)
  • Recently played games and timestamps (recently_played, last_played_times)
  • Play counts per game (play_counts)
  • Theme preference (selected_theme)
  • High scores and game progress (e.g. wordle_streak, snake_highscore)
  • Banner dismissal & consent choices (dev_banner_dismissed, cookie_consent)

This storage is used to provide core functionality you interact with (saving favorites, scores, preferences). You may decline non-essential storage via the consent banner; the site will still work, but preferences and scores will not persist between visits.

3. Third-Party Services

When you load this site, your browser connects to the following third parties. These providers receive technical data such as your IP address as part of any web request:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — used to load the "Outfit" typeface.
  • cdnjs / Cloudflare (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — used to load Font Awesome icons.
  • Flaticon CDN (cdn-icons-png.flaticon.com) — used for the site favicon.
  • iTunes Search API (via Apple) — queried by the Tunetile game to fetch short music previews.
  • Netlify — hosts the website and its serverless functions.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics trackers, or social media pixels.

4. Feedback Emails

The "Report a Bug" and "Suggest a Game" features open your own email client with a pre-filled message addressed to us. The pre-filled body includes the message you write, plus your browser user-agent string and the current date/time, to help us reproduce bugs. No data is sent until you choose to send the email yourself. If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message solely to respond to your feedback.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  1. Right of access — you can view everything we store about you directly in your browser's developer tools (Application → Local Storage), since all data is stored locally on your device.
  2. Right to erasure — you can delete all stored data at any time using the button below, or by clearing your browser's site data.
  3. Right to rectification & objection — since data is local, editing or deleting it in your browser exercises these rights instantly.
  4. Right to withdraw consent — you can change or revoke your storage consent choice at any time by clearing the cookie_consent entry or contacting us.
  5. Right to lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority.

This permanently removes all locally stored favorites, play history, high scores, theme preference, and consent choices for this site.

6. Children's Privacy

The Site is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 16. Since no accounts exist and no data is transmitted to us, no processing of children's data takes place.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
