How to Play

Guess the mystery target country in 6 attempts or fewer!

  • Distance: Shows how far your guess is from the mystery target.
  • Direction: Points toward the target country's location.
  • Proximity: Represents closeness percentage (100% = Correct).
  • Hints: Use up to 2 hints per game (continent, first letter).
  • Endless Mode: Play unlimited rounds with random countries.
  • Difficulty: Easy shows proximity, Hard hides direction & proximity.
