How to Play

🎯 Objective

Clear all 8 trivia categories in a single run without running out of lives!

🗺️ The Gauntlet

  • Each stage is a new category with escalating difficulty (easy → hard)
  • Answer the required number of questions correctly to unlock the next stage
  • General Knowledge → Video Games → Film → Science & Nature → Computers → Geography → Mythology → History

❤️ Lives & Clock

  • You start with 3 lives — wrong answers and timeouts cost one
  • A wrong answer does not reset your stage progress
  • Faster answers earn bigger time bonuses; harder questions are worth more points

🛟 Lifelines

Once per run: 50/50 removes two wrong answers, and Skip jumps to the next question without penalty.
