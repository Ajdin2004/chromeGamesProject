How to Play

Objective

Find as many words as you can on the 4×4 board before time runs out. Words need 3+ letters.

Controls

Click adjacent letters (or drag) to string them together, then Submit. Longer words score more.

Modes

Pick Easy (4m), Medium (3m) or Hard (2m). Use Hint for a clue or Shuffle for a fresh board.
