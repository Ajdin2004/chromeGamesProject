How to Play

Round 1 — Rank the Stat

Drag each tile with the handle (or use the ▲▼ buttons) to order the four items from highest to lowest value. 85 pts for every item in the correct slot.

Round 2 — Find the Outlier

Tap the one of four items that does not fit the day's hidden rule. 330 pts if correct.

Round 3 — Target Guess

Drag the slider to guess the exact stat. The closer you are, the more of 330 pts you earn — an exact match scores the max.

Daily reset

A new shared puzzle unlocks at local midnight (00:00). You can only play once per calendar day, and your streak is tracked automatically.
