How to Play

🎯 Objective

Guess today's mystery character in 8 attempts or less!

📊 Attributes

Each guess reveals how close you are across 6 attributes:

✓ Correct ◐ Partial ✗ Wrong
  • Correct — exact match
  • Partial — shares at least one value
  • Wrong — no match

💡 Hints

After 4 guesses, a hint unlocks with the character's release version and first letter.

♾️ Endless Mode

Switch to Endless mode to play unlimited rounds! Each round picks a random character. Endless games don't affect your daily stats.
