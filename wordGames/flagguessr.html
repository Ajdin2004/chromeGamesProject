How to Play

🎯 Objective

Guess today's mystery country from its flag in 6 attempts or less!

🌍 Modes

  • Daily — One flag per day, saved progress
  • Endless — Unlimited rounds, track your streak
  • Quiz — 10 quick multiple choice questions

💡 Hints

Use up to 2 hints per game:
• Hint 1: Continent
• Hint 2: Capital city + first letter

📊 Feedback

After each guess, the country is added to the list with ✅ or ❌. Wrong guesses get a related hint showing their continent and distance to the target.
